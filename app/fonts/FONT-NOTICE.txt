Fonts bundled with this app
============================

display.ttf — Noto Sans Condensed Black (used for the cover lettering, as "Vice Display")
ui.ttf      — Noto Sans Bold (used for the editor and small cover text, as "Vice UI")

Both are from Google's Noto font family and are
licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which allows them to be
bundled and redistributed with this app. The full license text is widely
published by SIL International; the fonts are unmodified apart from their file
names, shortened so the app's paths stay simple.
